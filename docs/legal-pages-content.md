By ticking the box to agree to these Terms & Conditions and submitting an application, you accept these terms, including the declarations and consents in section 5.

We recruit on behalf of employers ("our clients"). When you apply for a job on this site, you are applying through us. We review your application and, if it is suitable, we put you forward to the employer for the role.

## 2. What this website is for

You can use this website to:
- view current job openings
- apply for a job by completing the online application form and uploading supporting documents
- contact us with an enquiry

## 3. Who can apply

To apply, you must:
- be at least 18 years old
- give information that is true, complete and your own

Once an application has been submitted, you cannot edit it yourself. If something needs correcting, contact us at info@3deesrecruitment.com and quote your application reference number.

### 5.1 Accuracy of information
By submitting my application, I declare that:

1. All the information I have given in this application is true, complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge.
2. I have not left out any information that could reasonably affect the decision on my application.
3. Every document I have uploaded is genuine, belongs to me, and has not been altered in any way.
4. I am applying for myself, not on behalf of anyone else.
5. Any referees I have named have agreed to be contacted about me.

- my application may be rejected
- any job offer may be withdrawn
- if I have already been employed, my employment may be ended

*This declaration applies only to jobs that need it.*

Where the role requires it, I declare that, to the best of my knowledge, I am physically and mentally fit to carry out the duties of the role I am applying for.

If anyone asks you for money in the name of 3DEES or any of our clients, do not pay. Report it to us at info@3deesrecruitment.com.

## 8. How we share your application

# PAGE 2 — Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy**
*Last updated: [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]*

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or apply for a job, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it. It is written to meet the requirements of the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** and the **NDPA General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 (GAID)** issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

**3 Dees Consulting Works & Resourcing Ltd** ("3DEES", "we", "us") is the data controller for the information you give us on this website.

Address: [[REGISTERED ADDRESS]]
Email for privacy questions and requests: info@3deesrecruitment.com

## 2. Information we collect

**When you apply for a job**, we collect the information the application form asks for. The exact questions depend on the job, but may include:

- **Identity and contact details** — your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, nationality, state of origin, local government area, and residential address
- **Education** — schools attended, qualifications, grades and years of graduation
- **Work history** — past employers, job titles, dates and responsibilities
- **Professional details** — certifications, licences, memberships and skills
- **Referees** — names and contact details of people who can vouch for you
- **Job-specific answers** — any extra questions particular to the role
- **Health information, where a job requires it** — whether you are physically fit for the role, your medical fitness declaration, and a medical certificate. During verification, medical and drug test results may also be added to your application
- **Criminal record information, where a job requires it** — your answer to whether you have a criminal record. During verification, a police check report may also be added to your application
- **Documents** — files you upload, such as your CV, certificates, identification documents and photographs
- **Your declarations and consents** — a record that you agreed to these terms (including the declarations in section 5) and this policy, your consent to background and reference checks, and, where a job asks for it, your declaration that you are medically fit for the role

**When you contact us** through the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, organisation name (if given) and your message.

**When you visit the website**, our servers and security providers automatically record technical information such as your IP address and the time of your visit. We use this to keep the site secure and to stop abuse, for example by limiting how many applications can be sent from one connection. The website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking on this website. Our hosting and security providers may use small, strictly necessary files (cookies) to protect the site from attacks.

Health and criminal record information are especially sensitive. We only collect them for jobs where the employer needs them to decide whether you can safely and lawfully do the role, and the application form shows which ones a job asks for. We collect them with your explicit consent, limit who can see them, and share them only with the employer for that job.

We ask for your state of origin and local government area because employers commonly require them. [[LAWYER: confirm this explanation is adequate]]

We do not ask about your religion or political opinions.

## 3. Why we use your information

| What we do | Why we're allowed to |
|---|---|
| Review your application and decide whether to put you forward for the job | Your consent, and steps you have asked us to take before a possible employment contract |
| Send your application to the employer for that job | Your consent |
| Check that your information and documents are genuine | Your consent, and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud |
| Carry out background and reference checks | Your consent, given on the application form |
| Assess health and criminal record information, where a job requires it | Your explicit consent, given on the application form |
| Email you about your application | Our legitimate interest in keeping you informed |
| Reply to contact form enquiries | Our legitimate interest in responding to you |
| Keep the website secure and prevent misuse | Our legitimate interest in protecting our systems and users |
| Keep records we are required to keep, or respond to lawful requests from authorities | Legal obligation |

All decisions about your application are made by people. We do not use automated systems to accept or reject applicants.

## 4. Who we share your information with

**Our staff.** Only 3DEES staff who are authorised to handle recruitment can see your application.

**The employer.** If your application passes our review, we send it — including your documents — to the employer for that job. At present our employer client is **ECO Green Investments Limited**. The employer uses your information to decide whether to accept you, and if they accept you, they keep their own copy of your application and documents. From that point, the employer is also responsible for how they handle that copy, under their own privacy policy.

**Service providers.** We use trusted companies to run this website on our behalf — for hosting, database storage, secure file storage and sending email. They may only use your information to provide their service to us, and must keep it secure.

**Authorities.** We will share information with government or law enforcement bodies only where the law requires us to.

**We never sell your personal information**, and we do not share it for marketing.

## 5. Information stored outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers store or process information on servers outside Nigeria [[CONFIRM HOSTING REGIONS]]. When this happens, we rely on the safeguards allowed under Part VIII of the NDPA, such as contractual commitments from the provider to protect your information to a standard at least equal to Nigerian law.

## 6. How we protect your information

- Information sent between your device and our website is encrypted.
- Uploaded documents are kept in private storage. They are never publicly available, and staff open them using links that expire after a short time.
- Staff sign in with a password and a one-time code sent to their email.
- Important actions on applications — such as approving or rejecting — are recorded with the name of the staff member who took them.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and, where required, notify you.

## 7. How long we keep your information

The NDPA requires that personal information is kept no longer than is needed for the purpose it was collected for. On that basis:

- **Applications** are kept while the recruitment process for that job is running, and for up to **12 months** after it ends, so that we can answer questions or handle disputes about the process. After that, we delete your application and documents.
- **Contact form messages** are kept for up to **12 months** after we have dealt with your enquiry.
- We may keep a short record that an application was made and what decision was taken, for as long as we need it to deal with a legal claim. This record does not include your documents or the answers on your form.

If you are hired, the employer keeps the information it needs under its own policies.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner — see section 8.

## 8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your information is used — this policy does that
- **Access** the information we hold about you and receive a copy
- **Correct** information that is wrong or incomplete
- **Delete** your information where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it
- **Restrict** how we use your information in certain cases
- **Object** to us using your information on the basis of our legitimate interests
- **Data portability** — receive your information in a commonly used format, or have it sent to another organisation
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time

To use any of these rights, email info@3deesrecruitment.com with your full name and application reference number. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will respond within [[30 days]].

Please note: withdrawing your consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it. If your application has already been sent to an employer, we will tell them about your request, but you may also need to contact them directly about their copy.

## 9. Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first at info@3deesrecruitment.com so we can try to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** — www.ndpc.gov.ng.

## 10. Age

This website is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly accept applications from anyone younger. If we find that we have, we will delete the information.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will make it clear on this page.
